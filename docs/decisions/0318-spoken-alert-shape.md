# 0318: Spoken alert shape

Decision: a spoken alert is `<project> issue <n>, <short title>, <verb>`, with all-capitals words of two or
more letters lower-cased in the whole sentence. Long titles were slow to hear and capitals were spelled out.
The short title is about five words, cut at a word boundary, tail dropped after a colon, dash or parenthesis.
Only the spoken sentence changes; visual alerts keep the full title. Acronyms lose their capitals in speech
only; mixed-case words and single letters are unchanged.
