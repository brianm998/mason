# 0027: Showing live step progress, the choices the ticket left open

Task #14 (GitHub #21). Each is the reading taken; change one and the others stand.

- **Percent is floored and capped.** `percent(done, total)` is `floor(done * 100 / total)`, at most 100, none for a total of 0 (`mason_core::progress::percent`; the same rule written again in Swift and Kotlin and tested in each). 212 of 340 is 62%, 3 of 7 is 42%, 999 of 1,000 is 99%, and a count past its total reads 100%. Rounding up would show "100%" for a step that is not done.
- **A percentage needs a real total and a count.** Only `reported` or `counted` totals give one. The `history` total gives "1,240 lines, usually 1,800 to 2,400"; with a low end equal to or above the high end, just "usually 2,400". No total gives the count alone. A total of 0 is treated as no total.
- **A running cell with no total is an outline only.** The half fill that the cell drew for any part cell is removed: a half fill with no count said something the data could not back. A real total fills in proportion; a history total fills the same way at 40% opacity (lighter), capped at the full width.
- **"Quiet" is judged on `updated_at_ms`** (anything in the record moving, not only output), against `stale_after_ms`. The threshold is the high end of the middle half of the longest quiet gap of each past successful run of the step in this project (`step_history.gap_ms`), with at least five samples (`MIN_SAMPLES`) and never under 30 s (`MIN_STALE_MS`, so a step that used to chatter every 200 ms is not called silent after a second); with fewer than five samples it is 300 s (`DEFAULT_STALE_MS`). A run's longest gap is tracked while its output arrives (`Progress::max_gap_ms`, from the run's start to the first output, then between outputs) and stored with the record. The time text switches from "updated 4 s ago" to "no output for 6 min" once the gap is larger than the threshold. It is amber, never red.
- **The clock.** A monitor keeps `daemon_now - local_now` from `Welcome.now_ms` and each `StepProgress.now_ms`, and counts `local_now + offset - updated_at_ms`. A one-second timer redraws only the time text; no label contains it, so a screen reader is not told every second. A reconnect takes a new offset from its `Welcome`.
- **The live log keeps 40 lines.** `StepProgress.log` carries the whole lines that arrived since the last push, at most 40 (`LOG_KEEP`; older ones are dropped if a burst is larger). The daemon cuts lines per stream and holds an unfinished last line until the rest arrives (and drops one longer than 8 KB). A monitor appends them to a buffer of the last 40 and starts it again from `log_tail` when a task-detail view arrives, so a reconnect falls back to the snapshot. The log lines are not stored.
- **Todo has no source yet.** `ProgressCell.todo` and `Progress::todo` exist and are shown; `Daemon::progress_todo` is the entry point. Nothing real feeds it until the agent-count ticket (#19) lands; the demo seeds one. It is not stored, so a daemon restart loses it until the next report.
- **Migration 13**: `step_history.gap_ms` and `step_progress.max_gap_ms`, both `NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`. Old history rows have a gap of 0, which counts as a sample of 0 and so gives the 30 s floor until five newer runs exist. `total_low`, `history_samples` and `stale_after_ms` are offered again when a run begins and are not stored.
- **Protocol stays at 1; new numbers only**: `ProgressCell` 16 `subs` (`ProgressSub`), 17 `todo` (`TodoItem`), 18 `total_low`, 19 `history_samples`, 20 `stale_after_ms`; `StepProgress` 7 `log`.
- **Items for conformance.** One `progress:{task}:{step}` item per running step with a live record, from the sidebar rows and the task page: the count, the message, "count from the agent" / "total usually, from 12 past runs", "output N lines, N bytes", each sub-count as "name: count", each todo as "status: text", and `silent` when quiet. There is no clock text, so the dumps of the daemon and both monitors are the same whatever time they are taken. "Quiet" is judged at the time of the dump against the system clock (the monitors against the daemon's clock from the welcome); the demo's quiet step is 20 minutes quiet against a 5 minute threshold and its others are seconds old, so none is near the edge during a run of seconds.
- **The demo shows six cases at its last two checkpoints**, seeded as in-memory records (the demo has no step runs and no ticker): real count, history total, no total, quiet, sub-counts and todo. Their times are relative to when they are seeded, so left running for over five minutes the "fresh" ones go quiet too, as they would for a step that stopped.
- **CLI.** `mason task show` prints a `progress` line per running step (and its sub-counts, todo and output figures indented). `--watch` on `task show` and `status` opens a WebSocket (not command-only), subscribes to the task's detail or the sidebar, and prints a line for each `StepProgress` and the log lines that came with it; `task show --watch` ends when the task is over. `--json` prints the record set (`task show --json` carries `progress.runs`, the same figures as the push); with `--watch` each push as its protocol JSON. `mason task progress` keeps working and reads the same words.
