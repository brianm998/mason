# 0311: exit scripts follow the share rule; no tie-break by steps left

Status: accepted, pending the owner's approval of the trust-file (scripts/exit/**) changes.

Context: s08 and s09 asserted that the task with fewer steps left is admitted first. Since decision 0231 and #287 admission ranks by share of the route, and two fresh tasks tie at 1000 whatever their route length, so filing order decides. A daemon test asserts that.

Decision: keep the rule and its test; change s08 and s09 to assert it (equal share: filed first goes first). A tie-break in crates/scheduler/src/rank.rs by raw steps left was tried and rejected: it fails the scheduling test, and passing it would mean weakening that test. The other edits (s129, s165, s166, s08, s09) follow code that moved or remove clock and machine dependence; no check was deleted or loosened. The one removed line, `[ "$took" -lt 40 ]` in s09, was a wall-clock assertion; the bounded wait remains.
