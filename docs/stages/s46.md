# s46: Live step progress shown in both monitors and the CLI

Summary: both monitors and the CLI show each running step's live progress: a percentage only from a real count, the count and the typical range from history, the count alone otherwise, and always how long ago the step last moved, counting up on the monitor's clock between pushes and turning amber ("no output for 6 min") when it is quiet past its usual gap.

Task #14 (GitHub #21), the fourth of four tickets on live step progress. The counts of #19 (agents) and #20 (other step kinds) will show here as they land; the display does not need them.

## Built
- **The rule for numbers** (`mason_core::progress_text`, written again in `Format.swift` and `Format.kt`): real total gives "212 of 340 tests, 62%" (floored, at most 100); a `history` total gives "1,240 lines, usually 1,800 to 2,400" and no percentage; none gives "37 tool calls". Sources in words: "from the agent", "counted from output", "usually, from 12 past runs". DESIGN "Progress you can trust" (both copies) and the `progress-cells` description in `clients/features.toml` say this in place of "no percentages".
- **Protocol (additive, PROTOCOL stays 1)**: `ProgressCell` 16 `subs`, 17 `todo`, 18 `total_low`, 19 `history_samples`, 20 `stale_after_ms`; messages `ProgressSub`, `TodoItem`; `StepProgress.log = 7`. `mason.pb.swift` regenerated with `scripts/gen-clients.sh`.
- **Core** (`progress.rs`, pure): `percent`, `stale_after` (high end of the middle half of past runs' longest gaps, 30 s floor, 300 s with under five samples), `is_stale`, the run's longest gap, whole-line cutting per stream with the last 40 kept, todo items, `total_low`/`history_samples`.
- **Store, migration 13**: `step_history.gap_ms`, `step_progress.max_gap_ms`; `history_gaps`.
- **Daemon**: cells carry the new fields; `begin` offers the range and the stale threshold; pushes carry the new log lines; `progress_todo`; `GET /tasks/{id}` carries `progress` (`{now_ms, runs}`, the same figures as the push); the demo seeds six cases at its last two checkpoints (real count, history total, no total, quiet, sub-counts, todo).
- **CLI**: `mason task show` prints a `progress` line per running step (+ sub-counts, todo, output); `task show --watch` and `status --watch` follow `StepProgress` over a subscribed WebSocket; `--json` carries the records. `task progress` reads the same words.
- **SwiftUI and Compose**: the model keeps a clock offset (welcome, each push), applies `StepProgress` to the task page and the sidebar rows without a view, and keeps a 40-line live log (restarted from `log_tail` on each task view, which is the reconnect path). Cells fill in proportion (real), lighter (history) or stay an outline (none); amber when slow or quiet. A progress line per running step in the sidebar and on the task page (count, message that goes first when narrow, time on a 1 s ticker); the task page adds sub-counts, message, todo with status, output figures and sources, and the step line shows count and time. The cell's accessibility label reads "develop, running, 3 of 7 plan steps, 42 percent"; the ticking time is in no label (Swift: ignored children with a count-and-message label; Compose: cleared semantics).
- **Conformance**: `progress:{task}:{step}` items from sidebar rows and the task page, identical in the Rust tool and both clients, no clock text.
- Decision record: `docs/decisions/0024-live-progress-display-readings.md`.

## Tests
- core: `percent` (floor, cap, no total), the stale rule, gaps and log lines, the display strings (`progress_text`: real, typical, none, grouping, durations, time and quiet text).
- store: `history_gaps`; the stored record round-trips with its longest gap.
- CLI: the progress line for each case, quiet, sub-counts and todo.
- conformance: the item texts for each case, and a planted difference is a difference.
- Swift (35 tests pass, 6 new) and Compose core (38 pass, 6 new): count text for each case, cell fill, the stale rule and time text, the spoken label, `StepProgress` applied to the page and rows with the clock offset and the 40-line log, items without clock text.

## What testing found
- The first design stored nothing for the longest gap, so a restarted run would have started its gap at 0; `step_progress.max_gap_ms` fixes that.
- Without a floor, a step that printed every 200 ms would call itself silent after a second; the threshold has a 30 s floor (decision 0024).
