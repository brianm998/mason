# s989: a fixed red develop moves the tasks it held up

Summary: a background watch reruns a stale red baseline's tests narrowed at develop's tip and records a partial baseline, so a fixed test stops being "red on develop"; it folds develop into the developing tasks that failed those tests and tells their agents on the next `run_check`, alerts the owner when a branch stays behind 30 minutes, and checks a test that fails unexplained in three tasks at the tip (red there: recorded and one urgent task; green: the tasks are behind).

Decision record: docs/decisions/0982-red-develop-fixed-moves-held-tasks.md.

## What changed

- `crates/core/src/red_base.rs`, `crates/daemon/src/rerun.rs`: libtest's `--report-time` FAILED lines are read (commit e3171d38a, cherry-picked).
- `crates/store`: a migration (`baselines.partial`, `red_sightings`, `red_notes`, `red_folds`, `red_fix_pending`); `put_partial_baseline`, partial-aware `latest_baseline`.
- `crates/daemon/src/redwatch.rs` (new): the pass, notes, suspects, alerts.
- `jobs.rs`: sightings after each suite check, notes before the result, fold count in the "tree changed while waiting" test.
- `daemon.rs`, `attention.rs`, `lib.rs`: the loop and the alerts.

## Tests

Store: partial rows (green, still red, new red, not a cache, not replacing a whole row) and the sightings/notes/folds/pending tables. `redwatch::tests`: fixed set, note wording, the three-task window, known/baseline exclusion, the 30-minute alert on a given clock, the Home line. No test asserts timing.

## Not verified

- Nothing ran a whole daemon with a real probe, fold and agent: `redwatch::pass`, `probe` and `deliver` are covered by compiling and by the tests of what they call (`fixsync::merge_clean` has real-git tests). The exit script is unit and store tests plus greps, not a real masond with a scripted agent.
- The wait class `base-red` on a developing task (task view, monitor) is not shown; Home gets the alert line instead of a new field.
- A `run_check` already running (not queued) on the old tree when the fold happens is not stopped: it finishes, and the next check is on the folded tree. A queued one builds again on the folded tree.
- The probe needs the project's `rerun_filter`; without it nothing is narrowed and nothing is probed.
- A note reaches the agent only on a `run_check` result, not on other tool results.
- Probe runs hold no slots.
- "known_header says nothing" is covered through `latest_baseline` plus `red_on_develop_header`, not through `known_header` with a daemon.
- No fake-GitHub test of the placement modes.
