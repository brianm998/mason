# s401: a develop agent that keeps committing gets more time, and a timeout says how its time went

Summary: a develop step's deadline grows by a quarter of its budget each time it is about to end and the agent has committed or produced output since the last look, up to one extra budget in total; queue credit and progress grants survive a daemon restart (the cause of 60 timeouts on 2026-11-15); a step that still runs out of time says in its failure note how long it waited in queues, worked, and sat silent.

## What was measured

Source: the daemon's `store.db` events (`step_failed`, `wait_started`/`wait_ended`, `admitted`, `retried`) and `daemon-starts`, read read-only on 2026-11-15. s363 was committed 2026-11-14 17:27 PST (2026-11-15 01:27 UTC), so the post-s363 timeouts are the ones from 11-15 02:36 UTC on: **60 develop steps** ran out of time between 02:36 and 14:01 UTC.

| Group | Steps | When | Time after the latest daemon start | Queue time of the step (compile + test-suite + exclusive leases) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | #329, #320, #332 | 02:36, 03:09, 04:02 | 57, 15, 33 min | #320: 141 compile, 3 test-suite; #332: 118 compile (minutes, from the events) |
| B | #324, #333-#350 (20 steps) | 04:31-04:53 | 27-49 min after the 04:04 restart | 45-145 min of compile waiting each, plus 2-8 min of LLM slot |
| C | 40 steps (#325-#416) | 13:57-14:01 | 0.3-4.5 min after the 13:57:20 restart | 6 to 16 waits; 300-560 min of compile + test-suite + exclusive waiting each (#413: 303 + 211) |

Every one of the 60 failed within an hour after a daemon start, and 40 of them within 5 minutes of the 13:57 start. The daemon was restarted 13 times from 11-14 22:02 to 11-15 13:57. A step that waited this long in queues had a budget plus credit well above the time it had been running (#413: 555 min since its step began, 514 min of it queued, budget 240 + credit 514), so it was not out of time by s363's own arithmetic.

**Cause.** The host counts its limit from `started_ms` (s355/`remaining_limit`), but the credit s363 adds lives only in the daemon's memory (`DeadlineExtra`). A restarted daemon re-attaches to the host with the credit at zero, so a step that had been queueing for longer than its budget is stopped at once. Re-attachment is staggered by the scheduler, which explains the 0-49 minute spread after each start.

What the store cannot tell: how long each agent produced output or was silent. Agent output is not an event and the old daemon did not count it, so the output/silence split of these 60 is **not known**. The failure note now records it, but only for timeouts after this lands. Lease waits (`get_lease`) are neither credited nor measured. I did not read the agents' own logs. The 22 timeouts of 11-10 to 11-12 predate s363 and I did not break them down; many of them also cluster within seconds of each other (restarts again).

**Conclusion.** Queue time was the cause only through restarts: s363 credits it, and the restart discarded the credit. So the fix is to keep the credit: it is written to `deadline-extra-r<run>.kv` in the task directory each second when it changes and read back when the step is re-attached; the progress extender resumes at what it had granted. No budget default changes. The progress extension (budget/4 grants, cap one budget) stays for an agent that really works past the budget; no evidence in the store shows it was needed, and the cap bounds it.

## Change

- `crates/daemon/src/deadline.rs`: `Extender` (pure; clock, branch head and an output counter are passed in) and `timeout_note`.
- `DeadlineExtra` keeps queue credit and progress grants apart (`waited`, `progressed`; `get` is the sum, so both adapters' watchdogs are unchanged).
- `deadline::save`/`load`, `DeadlineExtra::restore`: queue credit and progress grants survive a daemon restart (test `credit_survives_a_restart`).
- `jobs.rs`: a develop step runs a one-second ticker that calls the extender with `git rev-parse HEAD` and a count of agent output chunks and events. A grant is made within 120 s of the end (or a quarter of a short budget). Each grant is budget/4; the total is capped at one budget, so working time is at most twice the budget plus queue credit.
- The timeout note reads: elapsed of budget, queue credit, progress grant, waiting, working, silence at the stop, commits on the branch.

## Not verified

- No real-agent run; the extension is exercised with the fake agent only.
- Lease waits (`get_lease`) are not credited and not measured; the note says so.
- Output counts as progress, so an agent looping with output but no commits is extended until the cap. The cap bounds it; if that proves too generous, drop output from the signal.
- "Silent" counts from the last agent event or output chunk seen by the daemon; a reattached session restarts the clock.
