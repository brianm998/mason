# s875: cargo-workspace force-skips are owner skips

Summary: The cargo-workspace force-skip count (18 of 81) comes from owner and Land-now skips, not suite failures, so the suite is unchanged and the detector's counting is filed as a proposal.

**Evidence (code, as on origin/develop).**
- `suites::skip` (crates/daemon/src/suites.rs:3918-3980) writes a `suite:<name>` debt when the owner skips a suite.
- `suites::defer_stopped` (suites.rs:5193-5212) writes a deferred debt per stopped suite on Land now.
- Nothing in suites.rs records a failed suite as a debt.
- The detector (crates/core/src/introspect.rs:552-562), fed by `force_skips_since` (crates/store/src/introspect.rs:393-410), counts every `suite:` debt alike.

**Evidence limit.** The reason text of the 18 stored debts was not read from the store. The owner-skip attribution is inferred from the code paths and the 2026-11-20 window, when testing was held and open tasks were skipped.

**Change.** None to the suite or to crates/. A proposal covers splitting owner/Land-now debts from failure-driven ones in the detector.
