# s1106: scan what a change adds for secrets and banned words

Summary: submit_change, run_check and landing now refuse added lines that hold a secret or a word on the owner's banned list, which lives outside git.

## Change

- `crates/daemon/src/contentscan.rs`: built-in secret patterns, the owner's lists (`<config>/banned-words.txt` and `banned-words/<project>.txt`, `MASON_BANNED_WORDS` for tests), masking, the `mason:allow-secret` comment and `masond contentscan`.
- Called beside `markers` in the submit validator, `run_check` and the landing job; a batch records allowed lines in `content-scan.log`.
- `markers::added_lines` is the shared reading of a diff. `regex` is a new dependency of the daemon (already in the lock file).
- `docs/self-hosting.md`: "Secrets and banned words".

The owner still has to write the list: until then only the built-in patterns apply. See decision 1095, which also lists what is not covered.

## Verification

See the submission for what was run. Not verified: the scan in a running daemon against the owner's real list, and `run_check`'s refusal path through a live agent (its scan is unit-tested; the call is one line).
