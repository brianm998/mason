# s109: approval steps and proposed tasks raise an alert

Summary: A task waiting at an approval step (`approval:<id>`) or sitting in `proposed` (`proposed:<id>`) now raises a `needs_you` alert that clears when it is approved, rejected or canceled, and is spoken as waiting for approval rather than for an answer.

`alerts_now` (`attention.rs`, a trust file) gains the two conditions; nothing about who may answer changes, and approving still needs the owner's identity. The daemon's `notify.rs` knows the two key prefixes and picks a new sentence kind (`approval`, `proposal`) for `core::notify::sentence`; the stored alert kind stays `needs_you`, so routing, sound and reminders are as for a question. A held (self-hosting) change is not covered; it already has its own handling.

Tests: `an_approval_step_raises_an_alert_that_clears_when_approved` and `a_proposed_task_raises_an_alert_that_clears_on_approve_and_on_reject` in `crates/daemon/tests/definitions.rs` (including the queued spoken sentence), a sentence unit test in `crates/core/src/notify.rs`, and `scripts/exit/s109.sh` (real daemon, `human-gated` template, `mason alerts` and `mason task approve`).

Not verified: the sound or speech on real hardware, and the remote channels (email, push) for these alerts; only the queued sentence is checked.
