# Stage 1161: step-host launch-listing tests report a failed launch

Summary: the step-host launch-listing tests now stop waiting when the launch ends and show its error, and they retry the ETXTBSY spawn race that most likely turned s594 red on ubuntu.

- `wait_listed` in `crates/step-host/tests/host.rs` waits until the launch is listed or its thread has finished; a finished, unlisted launch fails with its `io::Error`. The 10 s limit is unchanged.

- `launcher.rs` is unchanged (decision 1151).
