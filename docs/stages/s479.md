# s479: daemon tests no longer leak step hosts, agents or daemons

Summary: A test's `Scratch` now reaps the step hosts (and their agents) its data dir started when it is dropped, even on a panic, and the first scratch world of a test binary kills step hosts and test daemons whose `/tmp/ms*` dir is already gone.

`impl Drop for Scratch` calls the same verified `reap_verified` that `Masond` used, so only hosts recorded in that world's own `h/` registry are signalled. `sweep_orphans` (once per test binary) reads `ps`, and for a command with `--dir` or `--data-dir` under `/tmp/ms*` or `/private/tmp/ms*` whose scratch root no longer exists, kills it, its children and their process groups. Nothing is matched by name, and a real pipeline's hosts (other paths) never match. `tests/leaks.rs` starts a real masond with a `sleep 600` agent step, drops the world and waits (generous bound, no timing assertion) until no process mentions its dir.

Not verified: an in-process `Daemon` still alive when its `Scratch` drops could in principle relaunch a step after the reap; the sweep catches that at the next run. The sweep was not run against the leaked processes of 2026-11-15.
