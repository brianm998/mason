# s324: spoken alerts say "<project> issue <n>, <short title>, has <done>"

Summary: Spoken alerts now read "<project> issue <n>, <first five words>, <verb>" and lower-case all-capitals words so the voice never spells them out.

Spoken sentences (`sentence` in crates/core/src/notify.rs) put the verb after a short subject built by
`spoken_subject` (project, issue number, first ~5 words of the title, tail after `: `, ` - `, ` — ` or `(`
dropped, backticks and `#` removed). `speakable` lower-cases every all-capitals word of two or more letters
across the whole sentence. The monitors' visual text keeps the full title. The daemon's `subject_and_detail`
and the landed announcement build the new subject.

Not verified: how macOS `say` pronounces the lower-cased words; the Swift and Compose monitors were not run
(they speak the daemon's sentence verbatim).
