# s92: replay and world snapshots stop copying the world

Summary: Replay applies events in place, `Store::world()` returns a shared `Arc<World>` snapshot, appends mutate the writer's world without copying it, `verify()` uses its own connection, and `open_run` is an index, so restart time grows linearly with the log instead of with its square.

Decision record: `docs/decisions/0078-in-place-replay-and-shared-snapshots.md`.

## Change

See the decision record. Code: `reduce_in_place`/`Touched` and `World::refresh`/`open_runs` in `crates/engine`; `replay`, `append_one`, `Cmd::World`, `verify` in `crates/store/src/lib.rs`. `Daemon::world()` now returns `Arc<World>`; no other caller needed a change.

## Measured

`crates/store/examples/gen_log.rs` (`gen` then `bench`), release build, tasks of 13 events (the generator's lifecycle) with a 3 KB body, on this Mac (an iMac Pro, not idle). "Before" is the table in the ticket (14 events per task, origin/develop). Appends are one `Tick`, mean of 100 after the first; they include the SQLite commit, so they are noisy (one run showed a 6.6 ms mean at 58 tasks while another showed 0.6 ms).

| tasks | events | restart (replay), before | after | `world()` before | after | one append before | after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 58 | 755 | 0.08 s | 0.013 s | 0.19 ms | 0.04 ms | 0.46 ms | ~0.6-0.9 ms |
| 251 | 3,264 | 1.43 s | 0.019 s | 0.77 ms | 0.08 ms | 1.19 ms | ~0.56 ms |
| 1,001 | 13,014 | 25.8 s | 0.17 s | 4.2 ms | 0.12 ms | 5.7 ms | ~0.63 ms |
| 2,001 | 26,014 | 100.9 s | 0.47 s | 6.7 ms | 0.26 ms | 10.5 ms | ~1.3 ms |

`verify()` at 1,001 tasks: 0.18 s, no longer under the shared reader's mutex. Replay is now roughly linear in events apart from the per-event `refresh` visit of every task (read-only, no copy), which is the remaining quadratic term and is small.

## Tests

- `crates/store/tests/no_copy.rs`: no `World` clone during replay, `world()`, or appends with no snapshot held; snapshots are shared; a held snapshot is not changed by a later append.
- `crates/engine/tests/in_place.rs`: in place equals the pure `reduce`; refused events (including a part-way `BlockersChanged` cycle) leave the world equal; the open-run index.
- Existing golden-log replay, matrix, projection and `verify()` tests pass unchanged.
- Exit: `scripts/exit/s92.sh` (a real daemon over a generated 3,901-event database starts, answers `mason status`, and a new task lands).

## Not verified

- Behaviour against the live database, and the update self-test end to end with a large log.
- The rollback path (transaction failure after the world changed) has no test; it is a replay from the log.
- The "before" column is the ticket's own measurement, not re-run here; the generator's tasks have 13 events, not 14.
- The pollers' behaviour under many held snapshots (each append during a held snapshot copies once).
- #63 (writer loop and reader lock) was not present in this branch; a merge may conflict in `writer_run`.
