Summary: A failing s40 now names the card checkpoint keys held by only one of the owner and stranger captures and shows both capture logs, in which `conformance expected` records how each checkpoint settled.

Cause: not confirmed. s40 section 2 asserted that the two captures hold the same (checkpoint, card id) keys, said nothing about which differed, and discarded the captures' output. One candidate is `settle()` leaving by its 5 s quiet fallback before a card view arrives.
