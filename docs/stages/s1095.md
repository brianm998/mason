# Stage 1095: s40 says which card checkpoints differ

Summary: A failing s40 now names the card checkpoint keys held by only one of the owner and stranger captures and shows both capture logs, in which `conformance expected` records how each checkpoint settled.

Cause: not confirmed. s40 section 2 asserted that the two captures hold the same (checkpoint, card id) keys, said nothing about which differed, and discarded the captures' output. The candidate then was `settle()` leaving by its 5 s quiet fallback before a card view arrives. Decision 1025 has since removed that exit for demo daemons: they wait for the step's seq, so a demo capture ends at `target` or fails after 60 s.

Change: `conformance expected` prints `(settled: target)` or `(settled: quiet at #seq / daemon #target)` for each checkpoint. scripts/exit/s40.sh keeps each run's stdout and stderr in `owner.log` and `stranger.log`, prints their tails when it fails, and puts the differing keys and quiet exits in the assertion message. `quiet` names a live daemon's agreed round-trip exit. The settle conditions and the checkpoint JSON are unchanged.

Follow-up: if s40 goes red again, the differing keys show which card was missed; the cause is then not a quiet exit.
