# s459: the iPhone portrait layout, list then detail

Summary: the iPhone root is a navigation stack (sidebar first, a row pushes its page) over the Mac monitor's own pages in one narrow column, with a status line, banners, the session-limit stepper and tile taps, and speech stays off.

## What changed

- `PhoneRootView` (now `MonitorUI/Phone.swift`) is the compact-width root: `NavigationStack` bound to `PhoneNav`, banners above the list (`BannersView`), a compact status line in the navigation bar and a menu (project filter, add task, improve, mute). The model's page follows the stack, and a page the model opens itself (a link, a notification URL) is pushed.
- Pages (`PhoneScreens.swift`): home, task detail (decision options and Other, cancel, skip test, land now, blockers, plan, log tail), landing queue with its phases, usage, health, settings, and the section pages (active models, CPU, compilation, queued, exploring, developing, testing, review). They are the Mac's views; `HomePage`, `TaskDetailPage`, `SectionPage`, `StageRows` and `UsagePage` take `compact`, and `ActionButtons` stacks when its buttons do not fit one row. The old `Phone.swift` lists are gone (they tested for `skip`, which the daemon does not send; the action is `skip_test`).
- Count tiles scroll sideways; a tap pushes the lone task or the state's page (`PhoneLayout.tileTarget`).
- `MonitorModel.stepSessionLimit(by:)`: the Mac's and the phone's stepper both call it.
- `PhoneLayout.statusLine`, `hasPage`, `morePages`, and `PhoneNav.replace`, `follow`, `init(stack:)` in MonitorCore.
- Sheets use `sheetWidth` (fixed on the Mac, at most that wide on iOS) so a phone is not wider than its screen.
- `MonitorPhone` depends on `MonitorUI` and re-exports it. `scripts/exit/s123.sh` follows `Phone.swift` to its new path.
- Tests: `PhoneNavTests` (push and pop for every page, status line, tile target, stepper command) and `PhoneLayoutTests` (a row wraps and fits, actions and stepper fit, renders at 393×852 and 375×667). Decision record 0452. Exit script `scripts/exit/s459.sh`.
