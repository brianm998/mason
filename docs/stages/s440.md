# s440: top bar fits the minimum width with attention showing

Summary: the Swift top bar's "N need(s) you" label wraps instead of forcing the bar to 1079.6 pt, so it fits the 1000 pt window minimum again.

## Why

TopBarFitTests failed at the base: with attention showing the bar needed 1079.6 pt. The label was `.lineLimit(1).fixedSize()`, so it could not shrink.

## What changed

- `clients/swift/Sources/MonitorUI/TopBar.swift`: the label wraps (`fixedSize(horizontal: false, vertical: true)`). The tests and thresholds are unchanged.
- The Compose top bar was not changed.
