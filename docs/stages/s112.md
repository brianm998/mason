# s112: scheduling tests wait for the queue under load

Summary: `wait_explained` in the scheduling tests now waits on the broker's explanation under one 90 s deadline instead of panicking after a fixed 15 s, so they no longer fail on a heavily loaded machine.

The helper had a 15 s inner panic clock inside a 40 s `wait_for`. With load ~57 on 18 cores the scripted develop step took longer than 15 s and three tests failed on a clean develop. The timeout panic still says "never waited" and includes the task state and event dump.

Not changed: `wait_for` in `support/mod.rs` and the other per-wait budgets.
