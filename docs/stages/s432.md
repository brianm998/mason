# s432: pipeline self-correction: a scheduled health check and a health session

Summary: MASON now checks its own pipeline every `[health] interval_m` minutes (default 30; 0 off) and on demand (`mason health pipeline`); a pure rule in core turns gathered facts into findings, and a finding not handled within its cooldown starts one `health` owner session in a read-only checkout of MASON's own project, which ends in `submit_health` (false alarm, owner-safe actions, proposals, needs-owner, or a daemon-gated direct fix); a needs-owner session shows a `health` banner with an Open session button, and the sidebar lists every open session under "Active sessions (N)".

## Why

Each watcher saw one symptom (the event loop, idle compile holders, disk stalls, frozen launches, automatic retries) and did one thing. Nothing looked across tasks: many tasks past their time, a queue that did not move, the same failure on several tasks, diagnoses nobody applied, landing red for hours. The owner found out by looking.

## What changed

- `crates/core/src/health.rs` (pure): `judge(facts, limits)` gives findings with a stable key, a severity and evidence for the four groups (stuck, overloaded, failures not addressed, the daemon itself); `fresh` applies the cooldown; `cannot_land` is the daemon's own "nothing can land" condition.
- `crates/store/src/health_log.rs`, migration 40: one table, `health_log`, for starts, checks, handled findings, sessions, verdicts, actions, proposals and direct fixes. `mason health history` reads it.
- `crates/daemon/src/health.rs`: fact gathering (world, the published wait snapshot, the store, one timed file create; no broker lock), `tick(d, now)`, the loop (`[health]`), the session, `apply` for each verdict, the direct fix, the banner, the report and the history.
- `crates/adapter-api/src/contract.rs`: `StepKind::Health`, the `submit_health` tool, `HEALTH_ACTIONS` (retry, unblock, release_holder, rerun_diagnosis). An action outside the list is refused by validation.
- `crates/daemon/src/usersession.rs`: a session with no task and kind `health` works in its checkout, so the owner can write in it like in any session. Ending it removes the checkout.
- `crates/daemon/src/config.rs`, `main.rs`: `[health]` in `daemon.toml`: `interval_m` and every threshold (`stuck_factor`, `stuck_min_m`, `wait_m`, `queue_idle_m`, `host_silent_m`, `slot_wait_m`, `load_pct`, `stall_ms`, `frozen_launch_s`, `mem_free_mb`, `cpu_gate_m`, `undiagnosed_m`, `pending_diagnosis_h`, `same_failure`, `red_h`, `update_failures`, `restarts`, `late_loops`, `cannot_land`, `cooldown_h`). An unknown key is refused by name.
- `crates/daemon/src/views.rs`, `schemas/mason.proto`: `Banner.session`, `HeaderView.active_sessions` and the `ActiveSession` message (additive); a banner of kind `health` while a health session is open and not replying.
- `crates/daemon/src/http.rs`, `crates/cli/src/main.rs`: `GET /health/pipeline`, `POST /health/check`, `GET /health/history`; `mason health pipeline [--json]`, `mason health check`, `mason health history`. The existing `GET /health` and `mason health` (the detectors' report) keep their output and gain a `pipeline` key.
- Clients, Swift and Compose together: the banner's Open session button, the pinned "Active sessions (N)" panel (hidden at 0), and a minimal owner-session dialog (transcript, a field, Send, End, Close). `clients/features.toml`: `health-session-banner` and `active-sessions-panel`.
- Tests: core (every category, healthy facts, stable keys, cooldown), contract (`submit_health`), `crates/daemon/tests/health.rs` (one tick starts one session and the second none; `needs_owner` banner and its removal; false alarm; proposals and their dedupe; actions logged; the direct fix refused, allowed, and a protected path still waiting), client tests (Swift, Compose).
- `scripts/exit/s432.sh`.

## What I did not verify

- **The Swift client was not compiled or run.** SwiftPM needs to fetch `swift-protobuf` and this session may not reach the network (`fatal: transport 'https' not allowed`). The code follows the neighbouring views and I read it twice, but the Swift build, `OwnerSessionTests`, `ActiveSessionsTests` and the sheet are untested. The Compose core and app compile, and `OwnerSessionTest` passes.
- No XCUITests and no on-screen render were run.
- **The owner-session dialog did not exist in either client** (s364 shipped the daemon and CLI only). The sheets here are the smallest chat view that the banner and the panel can open; they are not designed.
- **The direct fix stops short of landing.** The daemon decides the "nothing can land" condition itself, makes the writable worktree on `claude/health-<id>`, lets the agent commit, finds protected paths and says so, and enforces one at a time and two failures a day. It does **not** push, land, or run fmt, clippy, the tests and the exit scripts on the branch: it hands the branch to the owner with `needs_owner`, and says so in the transcript. A route into the landing path that is both ahead of the queue and through every gate was not built, because it would be a new way for code to reach `develop` and I could not verify it here.
- `cpu_gate_held_ms` is read from the wait text ("cpu"), not from the CPU gate itself, and a task's slot wait is measured from the start of its state or its latest step run, because the broker's wait times are behind its mutex.
- The real machine's load, memory, `syspolicyd` and launch freezes were gathered but exercised only through fakes and a healthy machine.
- Two lints that were already red on this branch's base (`report.rs`, `owner_session.rs`) are fixed the way `origin/develop` has them.
