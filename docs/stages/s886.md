# s886: the gha ceiling test counted one job too many

Summary: The test other_workflows_jobs_and_the_ceiling_limit_what_mason_can_take now queues four MASON jobs under gha_ceiling 4 and asserts the fifth waits, instead of expecting five to be held.

**Cause: a fault in the test, not the scheduler.** With `gha_ceiling` 4 and no other jobs, MASON may hold 4 units. The test held `first` and `second` and then expected jobs 3, 4 and 5 to be held too, which is 5.

**Fix.** The range is `3..=4`, and a new assertion says the fifth job waits. No assertion was removed or weakened; production code is unchanged.

**Owner approval.** `crates/daemon/src/sched.rs` is in `PROTECTED` ("budgets: leases and limits"). Only the test body changed; this needs owner sign-off before it lands.
