# s417: an agent's queue waits keep counting for its deadline across a daemon restart

Summary: s363 added the time an agent's `mason run` commands wait in the queue to its step deadline. It added the wait only when it was granted, and kept the total only in the daemon's memory. Now an open wait is added every 30 seconds (`CREDIT_EVERY`) as it goes, and the rest when it is granted. The total is also written to `deadline-credit-r<run>.txt` in the task's folder, and a restarted daemon re-attaching to the session reads it back. A restart loses at most 30 seconds of credit per wait.

## Why

At 05:57 on 2026-11-15 the daemon took over from itself (a self-update). A re-attached agent's deadline counts from when its step host started (`remaining_limit`), but its credit started again at zero. Some develop agents had waited up to two hours for the machine, behind a `test-gui` run that needs all of it, and none of that wait had been granted yet. 29 of them timed out within four minutes of the takeover: 16 failed and 13 went back for another try. The owner retried the 16.

## What changed

- `crates/adapter-api/src/lib.rs`: `DeadlineExtra::kept_in(path)` reads the file, and `add` writes the new total to it.
- `crates/daemon/src/jobs.rs`: the agent step keeps its credit in the task folder per run.
- `crates/daemon/src/sched.rs`:
  - `Meta::External::credited`.
  - The scheduling pass credits open waits every `CREDIT_EVERY`, and the remainder on grant.
- Tests:
  - `credit_kept_in_a_file_is_read_back_by_a_new_handle` (adapter-api).
  - `an_open_mason_run_wait_is_added_to_the_deadline_before_it_is_granted` (sched).
  - The s363 test is updated for `credited`.

## Not covered

A `run_check` wait goes through `CheckSlots`, not the broker's external leases. Its credit is saved to the same file once it is added, but a `run_check` still waiting at the moment of a restart is credited only up to that point.
