# s181: the wait_share finding says what was waited on

Summary: The wait_share finding now lists what the waiting time was spent on (a slot, the owner, each resource) by share, so the cause of a high wait share can be read from the finding instead of guessed.

**Problem.** #204 reported 61% of active time spent waiting, pointing at `sched.rs`, but the finding carried only two totals. `charge()` in `crates/daemon/src/introspect.rs` already splits the time by reason ("a slot" for queued tasks, "the owner" for needs-attention, the lease's resource otherwise); the split was dropped before the detector.

**Change.** `Stats.wait_by` carries that split; the finding's evidence ends with `by what was waited on: a slot 70%, the owner 20%, ...` (top five). Thresholds and the measure are unchanged; no wait was reclassified or hidden.

**Not done.** No scheduler behaviour changed: without the breakdown there was no evidence which reason dominates. The next wait_share finding names it.
