# s902: a check's wait is credited every second

Summary: A develop agent's wait on a check (slot queue or GitHub) is credited to the step deadline every second instead of every 30 s, so the step no longer ends while a run is held queued.

The develop watchdog kills the agent at the deadline plus the credit. `TestChecks::queued` and `remote_credited` used `sched::CREDIT_EVERY` (30 s), so a check still queued at the 10 s budget had no credit yet and the step ended (s787 phase 1, #813). They now use `CHECK_CREDIT_EVERY` (1 s) in `crates/daemon/src/jobs.rs`. `sched::CREDIT_EVERY` and the `mason run` waits are unchanged. Unit test: `a_check_wait_is_credited_before_it_ends`.
