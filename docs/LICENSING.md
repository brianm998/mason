- **MASON itself** is GPL-3.0-or-later (`LICENSE`), with a draft section 7
  additional permission for app stores (`LICENSE-ADDITIONAL-PERMISSION`),
  awaiting legal review.
- **Output belongs to users.** Code, configuration and tickets that MASON or
  the agents it runs produce for your project are not covered by the GPL.
- **Templates and scaffolding** (`templates/`, and the `.mason/` files that
  `mason init` writes) are under the MIT licence, so copying them into a
  project creates no obligation. Files there carry
  `SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT`.
- **Plugins** run as separate processes over JSON-RPC and are not derived works
  of the core.
- This file is not legal advice.
